गणकवाटिका (gaṇakavāṭikā)
(lit.) a garden of computation.

Computation Garden collection of explorations (and corresponding stories about those explorations) into Computer Science.

The idea is to make complex ideas intuitive, to make CS as a subject approachable, and to make those ideas enjoyable to learn and understand. Learning for its own sake, if you will, without being constrained by some set syllbi.
